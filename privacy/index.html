Doormat: privacy and terms

Last updated 8 November 2026

The short version

What you photograph, paste or share into Doormat stays on your phone. There's no account, no advertising and no analytics: Doormat doesn't collect anything about you. Doormat can be wrong. If in doubt, ring the organisation yourself, on a number you've looked up.

Privacy

Your letters

Doormat reads letters, messages and screenshots on your phone. The photos, the text it reads and what it works out are never sent to us or anyone else.

The internet

Doormat doesn't connect to the internet by itself. If you tap a link to an official page or dial a number, your phone opens that page or makes that call as normal.

The person who helps you

If you choose someone to send letters to, Doormat keeps their name and number on your phone only. Doormat doesn't send anything itself. When you tap Send, your own Messages app (or whichever app you pick) does the sending, and you see the message first.

Reminders

If you tap Remind me, Doormat opens Apple's own screen for adding a calendar event. Nothing is added unless you save it. The reminder notification the day before comes from your phone, not from us.

Permissions

Doormat asks for the camera so it can read letters. Photos come through Apple's photo picker, so Doormat sees only the photos you choose. Contacts come through Apple's contact picker, so Doormat sees only the one person you choose.

Deleting

Everything Doormat keeps is on your phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Children

Doormat isn't made for children.

Terms

It's information, not advice

Doormat explains what a letter seems to say, using official pages, which it names for every answer. It isn't legal, financial or medical advice. For money worries, Citizens Advice, StepChange and National Debtline give free advice.

It can be wrong

A photo may be read wrongly, a letter type may be new, and an organisation's details may change. Doormat shows you where each answer comes from so you can check it. “This looks genuine” means Doormat found nothing that suggests a scam. It isn't a promise. Before you pay anyone or give your details, contact the organisation on a number you've looked up yourself.

Dates

Doormat shows a deadline only if the date is printed on the letter, or worked out from a printed date, and it says when it has worked one out. Always check the letter itself.

Phone numbers and links

These come from Doormat's own list of official contact details, never from the letter, and each one names where it came from.

No guarantee

Doormat is provided as it is. We're not responsible for decisions made using it, as far as the law allows. Nothing here affects your legal rights as a consumer.

Law

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.

Where the information comes from

Each answer in the app names its source, for example GOV.UK, Citizens Advice or the organisation's own website.
